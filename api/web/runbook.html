What this tool is (and is not)

It screens labels; you decide. Label Check reads a label image, compares it with the COLA application values, and shows evidence for every check. It has no approval authority — nothing is decided until you press a decision button, and your decision is what gets saved.

The six steps

  1. Add labels (top menu): images, a front + back pair, or a CSV batch.
  2. Watch the statuses: each application moves Received → Screened → Cross-checked → Your decision.
  3. Work the filters (bar under the menu): pick a commodity (Wine / Malt / Spirits), then a status — Needs attention collects everything waiting on you.
  4. Read the rows: label text vs application text, with a crop of the exact spot on the label. Hover the label image to magnify; click a crop to zoom.
  5. Decide: ✓ 👁 ✗ on each row as needed, then PASS / NEEDS REVIEW / FAIL for the whole label. PASS unlocks only when every row passes — machine-green or decided by you.
  6. Export results (CSV) or Save session — decisions are recorded with time and the original machine finding.

Reading evidence

  • Crops show the exact label region a check read. 🔍 means click to zoom.
  • Red boxes in a warning crop mark words that differ from the required text; dashed boxes mark where required words are missing.
  • NEEDS REVIEW is honesty, not failure — the tool refuses to guess when the image is blurry, small, or ambiguous. Your eyes are the resolution.

Known limits (by design)

Type size, exact placement, and contrast limits are not fully measurable from a photo (27 CFR §16.22; §4.36) — check those visually. Organic claims, formula approvals, and state-law variations are outside this tool's scope. When both OCR engines disagree, the field locks amber with both readings shown — that disagreement is yours to resolve.
